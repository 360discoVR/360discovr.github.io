What’s new

Changes in ART3M VR Player alpha releases. Existing testers get updates from the Meta Horizon Store automatically. How to join the alpha

1.30.14 24 November 2026

  • RTSP streams play again: before, they stopped after the first frame (any codec, 4K and 8K). 8K H.265 works over RTSP.
  • Stream codecs, honestly: H.264 over every protocol; H.265 (HEVC) over RTSP, HLS and DASH. RTMP and WebRTC take H.264 only. Help → “Connecting a stream”.
  • Report for the developer: the saved file now shows on the computer over the USB cable right away (before, the reports folder could look empty until a restart). If saving fails, the reason is shown under the button.

1.30.11 22 November 2026

New

  • Player panel: icons instead of words — tiles open the library, sliders open Advanced. A small ✕ above the panel hides it right away.
  • Advanced during a video: next to the controls or centred with a compact bar — library, ±10 s, pause, seek, volume, close. Settings → Playback → “Advanced during a video”.
  • Help in the headset: search with a “Search” key, topics (Get started, Picture or sound, Network, Expo), long articles scroll with Up / Down (by gaze too), “Open” jumps to the exact setting and highlights it.
  • Hand control: pinch thumb and index finger to click, like the controller trigger. In Expo mode hands don’t interfere with the show.
  • WebRTC (WHEP) — live streams with under a second of delay.
  • Controllers in the player look the same as in the Quest system.

Fixed and improved

  • Library: frames for 8K videos; clear tile states — Creating, No access, Frame unavailable; streams marked with an icon.
  • “Save for this video” tells the truth: saved on the headset, written next to the video, or failed — and the newest save wins after a restart.
  • The Expo wizard shows the same exit timing the player actually uses.
  • The usage journal and the developer report no longer contain addresses, file names, paths or what you type.

1.27.2 21 November 2026

  • The seek bar and value sliders land exactly where you aimed: the small jerk of the hand when releasing the trigger no longer moves them.
  • Meta Horizon Store purchase check (works offline).

1.27.1 16 November 2026

  • HLS, DASH and WebRTC streams work again; a dropped stream reconnects quietly under one waiting message.
  • Computer on the network: the guide is shown right away, the last folder opens directly, clear messages when Wi‑Fi is too slow, “Download to headset” right from the message.
  • Keyboard: delete key next to the digits, “Show” for passwords.
  • Simple mode never rotates videos.
  • Settings → System: anonymous usage journal and “Report for the developer”. What it contains and what it doesn’t — privacy policy.
