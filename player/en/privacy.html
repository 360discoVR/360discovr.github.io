Privacy Policy

Updated 25 November 2026. This policy covers the ART3M VR Player app for Meta Quest and this website.

In short

  • ART3M requires no separate account. The player has no ads, analytics services or tracking.
  • The usage journal and viewing statistics stay on your headset. You can turn off the journal or delete the files.
  • Simple mode is free. Meta checks access and handles the optional purchase of Professional and Expo. The player receives no card details.
  • We only receive data if you save a developer report and e-mail it to us yourself. We use it only to fix problems and improve the player.

1. Who we are

ART3M VR Player (“the app”) is developed and published on the Meta Horizon Store by the developer of 360discovr.com (“we”, “us”). For any privacy question or request write to artemkysliakov@gmail.com.

2. What data the app handles

The records below are kept on your headset. The player does not send them to the developer automatically. Section 6 explains network connections and Meta access checks.

Usage journal
Movies/ART3M/logs		Technical events: player version, headset model, system language, operating mode; which screens were opened, for how long, which buttons were pressed; playback quality (source type such as file / network / stream, start time, stalls, dropped frames, resolution, frame rate, bit rate, codec); Wi‑Fi band, link speed and signal strength; frame rate, memory, battery level and temperature; errors and unexpected closes. Never recorded: file names, stream or web addresses, IP addresses, computer or user names, passwords, PIN, text you type, the Wi‑Fi network name.
Viewing statistics
Movies/ART3M/ART3M_stats.csv		For booth operators: date and time, video title, type, seconds watched, whether it was watched to the end, operating mode. Also used to mark watched videos in the library.
Settings and your lists
Movies/ART3M		Player settings (including the operator PIN for Expo mode), the stream list you enter, per-video picture settings and notes.
Saved computers
app private storage		For “Computer on the network”: the computer's address and name, the user name, shared folder and last folder. The password is stored encrypted (see section 5).
ThumbnailsSmall preview images generated from your own videos, stored on the headset.
Hand trackingIf you control the player with your hands, the app reads from Meta Horizon OS only where each hand points and whether thumb and index finger are pinched, frame by frame. It is used only to move the pointer and press buttons; it is not stored, not written to the journal and not sent anywhere. Hand tracking can be turned off in the headset settings.

The app reads videos, photos and subtitle files that you place on the headset or open from your computer. It does not read your contacts, location, microphone, cameras (it never sees camera images) or other apps' data.

3. Developer reports you choose to send

In Settings → System → Report for the developer you can save a report file to Movies/ART3M/reports. It is a copy of the usage journal described above (no new data is added). The app never sends it: you copy it from the headset and e-mail it to us yourself, and you can open and read it before sending. When you e-mail us we also receive your e-mail address and whatever you write in the message.

4. How the data is used

  • On the headset: the journal helps diagnose problems you report; viewing statistics show operators which videos were watched; settings and saved computers make the app work the way you set it up.
  • Reports and e-mails we receive: only to find and fix bugs, improve playback quality and make the interface easier, and to reply to you.
  • We do not sell, rent or share this data, do not use it for advertising or profiling, and do not combine it with other sources.

5. How the data is protected

  • Minimisation: the journal stores types and numbers, not content. Every text line is cleaned before it is written: addresses, IP addresses, file paths, e-mail addresses and quoted names are removed.
  • Local only: the app has no analytics or advertising SDK and no connection to our servers.
  • Limited retention on the headset: journal files older than 7 days are deleted automatically, and the journal is capped at 20 MB.
  • Passwords: computer passwords are kept in the app's private storage, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore of the headset; they are never written to the shared Movies/ART3M folder or to the journal. The PIN pad and on-screen keyboard never record what you type (the journal only counts how many keys were pressed).
  • Network: the connection to your computer requires SMB encryption by default (Settings: “Encrypt computer connection”).
  • Reports we receive are kept in a private mailbox, read only by the developer, and deleted no later than 12 months after receipt, or earlier when no longer needed.

6. Network connections, access and purchases through Meta

The player connects to the stream servers (HLS, DASH, RTSP, RTMP, WebRTC) and computer shares you choose. Servers receive technical connection data, such as the headset’s IP address. Each server operator is responsible for handling that data.

The player uses Meta services to check access to the app and to the Professional and Expo modes for the current Meta account. It requests the current price in the store’s currency from Meta and unlocks the paid modes once purchase access is confirmed. If you choose to buy, Meta Checkout handles payment. The player receives access status and the purchase result. Meta handles your card details and other payment credentials under Meta’s privacy policy.

7. Your choices and how to delete data

  • Turn off recording: Settings → System → Usage journal; viewing statistics have their own switch (Viewing statistics) in Settings.
  • Delete on the headset: connect the headset to a computer and delete the Movies/ART3M/logs and Movies/ART3M/reports folders and ART3M_stats.csv. A saved computer and its password are removed with Forget in “Computer on the network”.
  • Uninstall: removing the app deletes its private storage (saved computers and passwords). Files in Movies/ART3M are your own files and stay until you delete them.
  • Delete a report you sent us: e-mail artemkysliakov@gmail.com with the subject “ART3M data deletion” from the address you used. We will delete the report and our correspondence within 30 days and confirm by e-mail. You can also ask what we hold about you.

8. Children

The app is rated for teens and adults (13+) and does not knowingly receive data from children. If you believe a child has sent us a report, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and describe important changes in the app's release notes on the Meta Horizon Store.
